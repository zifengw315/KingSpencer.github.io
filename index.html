Zifeng Wang

I am a Research Scientist at Google Cloud AI Research, focusing on large language models (LLMs) and their applications. My research interests span multi-agent systems, environment scaling, and agentic post-training. I received my PhD in Machine Learning from Northeastern University's SPIRAL Group, advised by Prof. Jennifer G. Dy.

Prior to my PhD, I earned my BS in Electronic Engineering from Tsinghua University, where I worked on computer vision with Prof. Jiwen Lu (Tsinghua) and Prof. Jia Deng (Princeton), and big data with Prof. Yong Li (Tsinghua).

I am looking for self-motivated student researchers / research interns with interests and expertise in LLMs! Feel free to contact me if you would like to work on cutting-edge challenges in machine learning and LLMs.
